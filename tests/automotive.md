## [Challenge] **Key Trends** in the Automotive Industry

The industry is being reshaped by massive technological shifts. EMQX provides the data backbone necessary to navigate these changes.

### Electrification

There is a growing shift towards electric vehicles (EVs), driving innovation in battery technology, powertrain systems, and charging infrastructure.

### Autonomous Driving

Autonomous or self-driving cars represent a significant trend with the potential to revolutionize transportation, enhance safety and efficiency, and transform the driving experience.

### Connectivity and IoV

Today’s vehicles are increasingly integrating IoV technology, improved where vehicles can communicate Intra-Vehicle, Vehicle to Vehicle (V2V), Vehicle to Infrastructure (V2I), Vehicle to Cloud (V2C), and Vehicle to Pedestrian (V2P). A real-time data platform enabling modern applications like collision avoidence, emergence vehicle priority and Cooperative Adaptive Cruise Control (CACC).

### Software-Defined Vehicles

As cars become more intelligence and dynamic, unlike the traditional vehicles with fixed harware-based systems, software increasingly drives vehicle functionality and performance, transforming industry towards greater personalized user expeirience and enabling predictive maintainence.

## [FAQ] Frequently Asked Questions
Get quick answers on how EMQX supports the complex needs of the automotive industry.

Q: How many electric cars does EMQX currently connect?
A: EMQX provides connectivity for over 20 million electric vehicles and connected cars globally, making it the proven data backbone for large-scale EV fleets and vehicle-to-cloud communications.

Q: What are the core benefits of using MQTT for automotive telematics?
A: MQTT is lightweight and efficient, reducing data transfer costs and battery consumption. It provides real-time, low-latency communication essential for vehicle tracking, remote diagnostics, and emergency services.

Q: Can EMQX support millions of vehicles simultaneously?
A: Yes. EMQX is a distributed, high-performance MQTT broker designed to handle millions of concurrent connections and route massive volumes of telemetry data per second, meeting the scale requirements of global OEMs.

Q: How does EMQX improve efficiency in automotive manufacturing?
A: By acting as a unified Industrial IoT (IIoT) data backbone, EMQX collects and processes real-time data from robots, sensors, and PLCs across the factory floor, enabling predictive maintenance, automated quality control, and optimized production flow.
